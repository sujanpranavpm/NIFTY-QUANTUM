### Correction 2 — Observed vs Simulated Backtest Returns

#### Evidence Consulted
- Requirement G1 in `P2.2.7_DATA_REQUIREMENTS_SPECIFICATION.md` previously contained ambiguous phrasing around `[OBSERVED]` inputs vs outputs.
- In `P2.2.7_CURRENT_DATA_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md` (row G1-G11) and `P2.2.7_RESEARCH_READINESS_MATRIX.md` (Capability 17 and §3), backtest outputs were described as "observed trades/returns".
- In `PROJECT_STATUS.md` and `PROJECT_HANDOFF.md`, resampling was described as resampling "the finite observed set".

#### Exact Taxonomy Implemented
Four distinct categories are now enforced across all documents:
1. **[OBSERVED] Market Data:** Market prices, quotes, and exchange prints (e.g. spot index bars A1/A2, option premium bars B1, settlement prices B6) taken directly from source data, subject to provenance, integrity, and quality checks.
2. **[SIMULATED] / [DERIVED] Backtest Trade Returns (G1):** Historical backtest trade P&L (`net_pnl`, `fees`, `slippage_cost`) and expectancy metrics produced by running strategy rules, execution/fill assumptions, and cost schedules over observed prices. These are model outputs, never observed market returns.
3. **[SIMULATED] / [DERIVED] Portfolio Equity Series (G2):** Daily equity curves and percentage returns produced by running the backtest accounting engine.
4. **Live-Trading Realised Returns:** Actual broker fills and realised account P&L under live market liquidity. None exist in this project today, and none are implied to exist.

#### Monte Carlo Engine Clarification
- The simulation engine resamples a finite set of backtest-generated simulated outcomes (G1/G2), not raw observed market returns.
- Running 10,000 bootstrap iterations provides numerical precision for confidence intervals; it does **not** create 10,000 independent historical market observations.
- Statistical confidence is bounded strictly by the number of independent historical market days and out-of-sample events, alongside model execution and cost realism.

### Correction 3 — India VIX vs Option Contract Implied Volatility

#### Evidence Consulted
- In `P2.2.7_DATA_GAP_AND_BLOCKER_REPORT.md` (§1.4, §2, §3), India VIX (C1) was listed alongside C2/C3/C4 as if index VIX were an input to Black-Scholes implied volatility inversion.
- In `P2.2.7_RESEARCH_READINESS_MATRIX.md` (Capability 11 and Section 2), India VIX was grouped under `RED-CONSTRAINT (impossible at ₹0)`.

#### Exact Corrections Implemented
1. **Mathematical Independence:** India VIX is an index-level composite volatility benchmark. It is **not** an input to Black-Scholes option pricing or contract IV inversion. An individual contract's implied volatility is inverted from *that contract's own premium (B1)*, underlying spot (A1), strike, and time to expiry (B4), alongside a risk-free interest rate (C4) and an index dividend yield assumption (C5).
2. **Missing Data Impact Separated:**
   - Missing **India VIX (C1)** prevents index-volatility-conditioned regime analysis and position sizing.
   - Missing **observed contract IV (C2) and Greeks (C3)** prevents research that specifically requires actual historical IV observations.
3. **Model-Derived Estimates vs Observed Data:**
   - Any IV and Greeks computed via option-pricing inversion are **[MODEL-DERIVED / ESTIMATED]** outputs, not observed market data.
   - They must be stored separately with derivation inputs, model choice, timestamp, and conventions recorded (Rule H5).
   - They do not constitute an observed market volatility surface, do not reflect real market skews or order-book pressures, and cannot be assumed suitable for every IV-aware strategy.
4. **Candidate Free Official Sources (for Later Discovery):**
   - **India VIX (C1):** Published historically by the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) on its official archives free of charge. Classified as `RED-DATA` (obtainable for ₹0 in a future authorized milestone), not `RED-CONSTRAINT`.
   - **Risk-free Rate (C4):** Official benchmark cut-off yields (e.g. 91-day Treasury Bills) are published by RBI and Financial Benchmarks India Pvt Ltd (FBIL) / CCIL.
   - **Dividend Assumptions (C5):** Historical daily NIFTY 50 dividend yields are published by NSE Indices.
   - *Note:* These sources are identified solely for future planning (P2.2.8); no searches, fetches, or downloads were performed.
