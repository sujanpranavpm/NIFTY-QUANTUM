# P2.2.5 — Initial Strategy Screening

Date: 2026-11-06 · Baseline commit: `03a5d27` (HEAD unchanged) · Continues from the completed
**P2.2.4** expanded working tree · Budget: ₹0.

**Objective.** Evaluate the five Tier-1 candidates on the expanded 265-expiry dataset using the
**unchanged, audited options adapter**, apply the pre-registered screening methodology, and
classify every candidate. This is *screening*, not a profitability claim and not a trading gate.

* **Candidates:** `orders_12`, `orders_01`, `orders_18`, `orders_05`, `orders_07`.
* **Methodology (authoritative, pre-registered):** `P2.2_BACKTESTING_METHODOLOGY.md` — periods,
  floors, metric definitions, scenario rules, statistics, baselines and the decision rule are all
  taken from it verbatim; none was invented or re-tuned here.
* **Harness:** `scripts/_p225_screen.py`, which calls `OptionTradeAdapter(AdapterConfig(...))`
  exactly as in P2.2.3/P2.2.4 and wires the evaluation through the existing
  `nifty_quant/validation/*` validators (`generate_folds`, `window_metrics`, `bootstrap_mean_ci`,
  `bootstrap_sharpe_ci`, `probabilistic_sharpe_ratio`, `deflated_sharpe_ratio`,
  `benjamini_hochberg`, `binomial_sign_test`) and `analytics.metrics.max_drawdown`.

> **Headline:** **zero candidates advance.** Four are **insufficient evidence** (below the fixed
> Development trade floor of 30) and one is **fails screening** (meets the floor but is not
> break-even and is sign-fragile). This is the outcome the methodology explicitly expects on this
> sample. No parameter, threshold, contract-selection rule or floor was changed to reach it, and
> the protected holdout was read **once**, at the end, and never used to decide.

## 2. Chronological periods (fixed a priori, unchanged from P2.2.2.1/P2.2.3)

Attribution is by **entry date** (the decision date), so a position that opens near a boundary
carries its risk into — and only into — the window where it was entered; this is the same
entry-in-window convention `walkforward.window_metrics` uses and is the leakage control.

| Period | Date range | Role | Trades (unique, BASELINE) |
|--------|-----------|------|---------------------------|
| **Development** | 2021-05-24 → 2024-06-30 | scoring, floors, thresholds | see §3 |
| **Validation** | 2024-07-01 → 2025-06-30 | walk-forward IS/OOS, robustness | see §3 |
| **Protected Holdout** | 2025-07-01 → 2026-07-02 (tail) | **evaluated once, never used to decide** | see §9 |

Boundaries are the pre-registered constants; the holdout tail now extends to the last expanded
eligible day (was 2026-02-24 pre-P2.2.4). **All thresholds were frozen before the holdout was
read** — the decision for every candidate is computed from Development + Validation only.

## 8. Walk-forward, confidence intervals and statistical tests

**Walk-forward** (methodology §2, `generate_folds` train≈6 mo / test≈2 mo / step≈2 mo, run over
Development+Validation only): every candidate yields **0 reliable OOS folds of 22** — no fold
reaches the ≥5-trade OOS floor (`MIN_FOLD_TRADES`). The walk-forward is therefore **unreliable by
construction** on this event density; reported, not hidden.

## 11. Data-quality limitations

* **Sample density is now the binding constraint, not data availability.** 121 unique entries over
  5 candidates on a 1,072-eligible-day calendar means per-candidate Development counts sit in the
  single digits to low 30s — at or below the fixed floors.
* **Long-only / CE-only.** Every candidate is bullish; none ever raises a PE, so results are
  one-directional by strategy design (not adapted, not manufactured).
* **Costs fail-closed.** `all_in_verified = 0`; brokerage excluded; pre-Oct-2024 statutory rows are
  estimated → those trades are *modelling only*, never strict-financial.
* **Exit fragility vs naive hold.** The fixed TP/SL underperforms hold-to-expiry ATM — the exits,
  not the signals, are the weak link on this data.
* **Identical signals for three candidates** (§3) limits how much the screening can discriminate.
