## 3. The one-paragraph verdict

**NIFTY QUANT can reliably run** a daily index-direction, long-only CE option point study on observables
(2024+ verified, 2021-2023 present-but-unverifiable), a fully-verified daily benchmark equity backtest,
price/vol-regime conditioning, integrity-checked 1m/5m/15m candles, and i.i.d. bootstrap/walk-forward/FDR
inference **on the simulated trade and portfolio returns produced by backtesting from observed prices**. It **cannot** — under the ₹0 / no-account /
no-download constraints — do spread-true or impact-aware execution, IV/Greeks-based strategy development,
or full-horizon strict financial validation (a permanent fail-closed gate). Between those two poles sits a
**largely closable** band: a real volume/liquidity gate and official-settlement wiring (no new data needed,
deferred by the "no execution modification" rule), denser option history for statistical confidence, and a
₹0-future full-universe + point-in-time equity fetch (T10) that today blocks survivorship-correct research.
This is the map for the *next* decision — external-source discovery and downloads are explicitly **not**
authorised until this audit is reviewed.
