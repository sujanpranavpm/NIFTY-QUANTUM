## 2. Definitions used (verified, not invented)

| Metric | Definition applied in the independent recompute | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Unique entries | distinct `(signal_date, expiry, strike)` per strategy, per period | trades |
| Gross P&L | Σ `gross_pnl` over `status ∈ {FILLED, OPEN_AT_END}` | ₹ |
| Statutory charges | Σ `total_costs` (brokerage **excluded** by construction) | ₹ |
| Brokerage-excluded net P&L | Σ `net_pnl`; identity `net == gross − costs` verified per row (§7) | ₹ |
| Expectancy ₹ | mean `net_pnl` per closed trade (Dev+Val block) | ₹/trade |
| Expectancy % | per-trade `net_pnl / (entry_premium × lot × lots)` then averaged (premium-denominated) | fraction/trade |
| Profit factor | Σ|winning net| / Σ|losing net|, `inf` if no losers, `nan` if neither — **cannot be negative** | ratio |
| Win rate | wins / closed trades (win: `net_pnl > 0`) | fraction |
| Drawdown | strict chronological equity curve on Dev+Val, expressed on the premium-equity base | fraction (negative) |
| Periods | Development ≤ 2024-06-30, Validation ≤ 2025-06-30, Holdout after — assigned by **entry date** (unchanged boundaries) | — |

Note on the two expectancy columns: they use **different denominators** (₹ cash vs premium
fraction), so their signs can legitimately differ for the same strategy (e.g. as-run
orders_12: exp_net **+235.1 ₹** but exp_ret **−0.04995**). The approved §5 break-even gate
reads **expectancy_net (₹)**. All such divergences are itemised in §7 and are *consistent
under the definitions* — they were the raw material the session summary garbled.

## 5. Why the tables look sign-mixed — and why they are not contradictory

Per §2, expectancy_net (₹) and expectancy_ret (premium fraction) can carry opposite signs
because heavy-loss trades and premium bases differ. Observed divergences (as-run
12/01/18: +₹235.1 vs −0.04995; as-run 05: −₹113.9 vs +0.02348; approved 07: −₹79.4 vs
+0.00314) were each re-derived from the raw rows and are arithmetically correct. The
qualification rule reads expectancy_net. **No PF cell anywhere in any artifact is negative**
(sweep of every `profit_factor` column: none) — a summary line quoting "−24 PF" conflated
PF with an ₹ expectancy figure and misattributed it to 5m.

## 6. Profit-factor sign audit

Standard definition `Σwins / Σ|losses|` is non-negative by construction. Verified two ways:
(a) re-PF from raw rows across both bands and all scenarios — all values in [0, ∞);
(b) cell sweep of every persisted metric table (`p225`, `p226` `scenario_metrics.csv`,
`exit_comparison.csv`, published `decisions.csv`) — zero negative PF cells. Where a table
showed `0.000`, that is the no-winning-trades case (numerator 0), which is correct.
