# P2.2.4 — Historical NIFTY Options Coverage Expansion (legitimate ₹0 sources)

**Status:** complete, awaiting review. **Not committed.** No raw data overwritten; original
dataset and Git history preserved (all new data lives in gitignored `data/raw`,
`data/derived`, `logs`, `artifacts`).

---

## 1. Objective

Expand the cached historical NIFTY-options intraday universe using only legitimate,
zero-cost sources, regenerate the derived option candles through the existing validated
pipeline, update the durable coverage registry without fabrication, and re-run the
**unchanged** P2.2.3 options-buying adapter against the expanded dataset — reporting the
before/after effect on usable coverage and on the opportunity set. No strategy, exit
rule, cost schedule or adapter default was touched.

remains excluded (never silently folded into a "net").

## 6. Data-quality observations (new and carried)

* **No new integrity regressions:** 0 raw schema/quality issues across all 267 cached
  files; candle integrity PASS (0 OHLC/conservation/print failures over 267 files).
* **Two expiries yield no 15m candles** — `2026-06-09` and `2026-08-04`. Their raw files
  are valid but every (day × expiry) block fails strict session classification (sparse /
  partial final sessions just before expiry, notably the truncated August-2026 tail).
  Reported as-is; not aggregated, not back-filled. This is why usable candles = 265.
* **1,005 rejected (day × expiry) blocks** across the cache — partial-session days
  excluded by the strict classifier (correct: they must not enter candles).
* **37 file-days stop before the close** (truncated sessions) and **346 contract-days sit
  in truncated sessions** — detected, reported, and excluded from candles.
* **9 real in-span provider holes** (2024+-listed monthly expiries the provider never
  publishes, e.g. 2025-12-24, 2026-06-25, 2026-06-30) and **37 beyond-horizon** future
  expiries. These cannot be filled from this ₹0 source; they are reported as `MISSING_FILE`
  / beyond-horizon, never fabricated. `PUBLISHED_NOT_CACHED` (the audit gap) dropped 244 → 0.
* **Registry ↔ completeness reconcile:** the registry's internal reconcile is `ok=True`;
  `logs/m5_completeness.json` (regenerated) sums to 179,426 exchange-enumerated cells,
  matching `test_coverage_registry`'s reconciliation assertion exactly.
