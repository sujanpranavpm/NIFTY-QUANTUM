## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This audit examines the 13 data domains (A–M) discovered during milestone `P2.2.8`. While several institutional portals and archives host historical Indian market data at ₹0 without authentication gateways, public technical accessibility does **not** equate to an express license for automated scraping, mass data harvesting, persistent storage, or downstream redistribution.

In milestone **P2.2.9.1**, the repository owner clarified that the access constraint is:
1. **Cost:** ₹0 absolute (no paid subscriptions, fees, or paid trials).
2. **Login:** No new provider-specific account. An existing Google account sign-in (e.g., Google OAuth or Google Drive sign-in) is **acceptable**. Sources requiring separate provider registrations, broker credentials, or KYC are rejected.
3. **Two-Axis Framework:** Technical access eligibility (Axis 1: PASS — NO LOGIN / PASS — GOOGLE LOGIN / FAIL — PAYMENT / FAIL — NON-GOOGLE) is strictly separated from data-use permission (Axis 2: PERMITTED / CONDITIONAL / PROHIBITED / NOT APPLICABLE). Google sign-in grants access, but does **not** confer copyright or data-use rights.
4. **Derived Domains (B & G):** Do not have separate external feeds, but strictly inherit the governance and licensing restrictions of their upstream inputs.

Each candidate source has been evaluated against:
1. **Public Terms of Service (ToS) & Disclaimers:** Restrictions on automated collection, bot access, reverse engineering, and archival retention.
2. **Access Mechanics:** Direct HTTP GET, browser-driven manual export, existing Google sign-in, or third-party scraping libraries.
3. **Data Quality & Continuity:** Schema transitions (e.g., NSE Unified Data Integration Format [UDiFF] effective July 8, 2024), missing fields, and historical coverage.
4. **Research Suitability:** Classification into four strict readiness verdicts:
   - `GO-CANDIDATE`: Verified open policy, free, no login or existing Google login, permits non-commercial internal research, clear provenance.
   - `CONDITIONAL`: Accessible at ₹0 without separate provider login, but policy restricts automated bulk scraping/harvesting or storage in retrieval systems without explicit written consent; manual/interactive export or formal policy clarification required.
   - `DERIVED-ONLY`: No external feed required or permitted; computed deterministically from already-governed base inputs within the repository, strictly inheriting upstream constraints.
   - `NO-GO`: Violates terms of service, brittle unofficial scraper, paywalled, requires broker/separate account, or redistributes unlicensed proprietary market data.

### Domain A: India VIX Historical Daily Data

#### Primary Source: SRC-A1 — NSE Historical VIX Portal
1. **Publisher & Canonical URLs:**  
   - Publisher: National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE India)
   - Canonical Portal: `https://www.nseindia.com/reports-indices-historical-vix`

2. **Login / Authentication Requirements:**  
   - None. Accessible at ₹0 without account, API key, or credentials.
3. **Terms of Use & Policy Clauses:**  
   - *Clause on Systematic Collection:* NSE India Terms of Use explicitly state: *"You may not conduct any systematic or automated data collection activities (including without limitation scraping, data mining, data extraction and data harvesting) on or in relation to our website without our express written consent."*
   - *Clause on Proprietary Rights:* Content is protected by copyright law and owned by NSE India. Personal, non-commercial viewing is permitted, but automated mass data harvesting violates site policy. Akamai bot-mitigation headers are actively enforced.
4. **Available Fields (CSV Headers):**  
   - `Date`, `Open`, `High`, `Low`, `Close`, `Prev. Close`, `Change`, `%Change`
5. **Historical Coverage:**  
   - Claimed: March 2009 to Present.
   - Verified: March 2009 to Present available via manual portal date queries.
6. **Cadence & Format:**  
   - Cadence: Daily (EOD, populated post-16:00 IST).
   - Format: CSV download via browser.
7. **Known Limitations & Caveats:**  
   - Browser cookies and anti-scraping sessions block direct headless scripts (`curl`, raw `requests`) with HTTP 403 Forbidden.
   - VIX is an annualized percentage volatility index (e.g., 14.50 = 14.50% annualized volatility), not a tradable equity price. It must not be scaled by 100 during feature generation.
8. **Proposed Validation Checks:**  
   - `Date` parsing (`%d-%b-%Y` to ISO-8601 `YYYY-MM-DD`).
   - Strict numeric bounds: $0.0 < \text{Low} \le \text{Open}, \text{Close} \le \text{High} < 150.0$.
   - Continuity check against NSE trading calendar (zero gaps on active trading days).
9. **Final Legal/Technical Verdict:**  
   - **`CONDITIONAL`**
10. **Confidence & Rationale:**  
    - Confidence: **HIGH**. Manual download via web browser for personal research is permitted under fair use/personal inspection, but setting up automated scrapers violates the express terms of use. Automated pipeline execution requires written consent or reliance on manual exports.

#### Secondary Source: SRC-A2 — Yahoo Finance (`^INDIAVIX` via `yfinance`)

- **Rationale:** Yahoo Finance Terms of Service expressly forbid automated scraping, commercial redistribution, and reverse-engineering. 4. **Calculated Fields:**  
   - `implied_volatility`, `delta`, `gamma`, `theta`, `vega`, `rho`, `moneyness` ($S/K$), `time_to_maturity_days`.
5. **Historical Coverage:**  
   - Entire scope where contract-level option quotes (Domain L/K), underlying index prices, and risk-free rates (Domain C) co-exist (2021–2024).
6. **Cadence & Format:**  
   - In-memory calculation / parquet persistence alongside processed analytical features.
7. **Known Limitations & Caveats:**  
   - BSM assumes continuous trading, constant volatility, and log-normal distributions. Deep OTM/ITM contracts with zero volume or extreme bid-ask spreads yield unstable or undefined implied volatilities.

8. **Proposed Validation Checks:**  
   - Put-Call Parity arbitrage bounds validation: $C - P = S - K e^{-rT}$.
   - Volatility bounds check: $0.01 \le \sigma_{IV} \le 3.00$ (1% to 300%). Return `NaN` if solver fails to converge within 100 iterations.
   - Delta sign consistency: $0.0 \le \Delta_{CE} \le 1.0$, $-1.0 \le \Delta_{PE} \le 0.0$.
9. **Final Legal/Technical Verdict:**  
   - **`DERIVED-ONLY`**
10. **Confidence & Rationale:**  
    - Confidence: **HIGH**. No external data licensing dependency exists. All Greek values are internal mathematical derivations and will be stored with explicit `derived_` prefixes to avoid confusing them with exchange-published data.
