## 1. Executive Readiness Decision

Based on the thorough legal policy, terms of use, and technical feasibility audit conducted in milestone `P2.2.9`:

1. **Overall Acquisition Readiness: PARTIALLY READY (CONDITIONAL)**
   - A viable, ₹0, non-commercial research workflow **is technically feasible** across all 13 discovered data domains (A–M).

While hosted on public servers and accessible at ₹0 without account credentials, their official terms of service explicitly prohibit automated data collection (scraping/harvesting) or storage in retrieval systems without express written consent.

   - **No automated scraping pipelines or unthrottled crawling scripts may be deployed against NSE India or Nifty Indices endpoints.**
   - All `CONDITIONAL` sources must enter the repository exclusively via **manual browser-driven exports** for personal academic research, or following explicit written clarification from data owners.
   - Ingestion must follow the strictly fail-closed technical validation plan defined in `P2.2.9_TECHNICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`.

## 3. Part E: Research Consequences & Honest Scope Boundaries

Quantitative research integrity requires clear transparency regarding what the data model can and cannot achieve.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| RESEARCH CAPABILITY MATRIX: UNLOCKED VS. PERMANENTLY BLOCKED                                                          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| What Acquisition UNLOCKS (Feasible at ₹0)                   | What Remains PERMANENTLY BLOCKED at ₹0                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Volatility Regime Modeling (India VIX & BSM ATM IV)      | 1. Level-2 / Level-3 Order Book Depth                   |
| 2. Multi-Day Risk Premia & Theta Decay Backtests            | 2. Microstructure Bid/Ask Spread Dynamics               |
| 3. Institutional Positioning Regimes (FII/DII Net OI)       | 3. High-Frequency Market Making / Order Flow Imbalance  |
| 4. Survivorship-Bias-Free Cross-Sectional Momentum          | 4. Sub-Minute Queue Position & Latency Simulation       |
| 5. Cost-of-Carry & T-Bill Adjusted Option Payoffs           | 5. Real-Time Dynamic Slippage & Market Impact Models    |
| 6. Macro Regime Filtering (Inflation & Monetary Stance)     | 6. Proprietary Broker Execution Algorithms              |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 What Acquisition Will Unlock
1. **Regime-Aware Volatility Strategies:** Integrating India VIX (Domain A) and model-derived Greeks (Domain B) enables dynamic sizing and IV crush exploitation around binary events (e.g., Union Budgets, Election results).
2. **Institutional Sentiment Overlay:** Participant open interest (Domain E) provides daily net exposure metrics for foreign institutional investors (FII) and proprietary desks, unlocking regime filters for index futures.
3. **Survivorship-Bias-Free Equity Research:** Combining Point-in-Time constituent histories (Domain I) with Cash Market Bhavcopies (Domain H) enables backtesting of cash and stock future strategies without historical survivorship distortion.
4. **Multi-Horizon Validation:** Combining 1-minute intraday options (Domain L) with authoritative daily settlement bhavcopies (Domain K) permits robust strategy backtesting across both intraday scalp and multi-day swing horizons.

### 3.2 What Remains Permanently Blocked Under ₹0 Budget
1. **Microstructure & Order Book Dynamics:** Without Level-2/Level-3 market depth, strategies relying on bid-ask queue imbalances, book pressure, or iceberg order detection cannot be tested.
2. **Sub-Minute Execution & Slippage Modeling:** Strategies relying on sub-second fill latency, maker-taker rebates, or tick-level execution cannot be evaluated. The backtest engine must enforce conservative fixed-tick slippage penalties (e.g., 1–2 ticks minimum per execution).
3. **Automated Continuous Real-Time Ingestion:** Because automated web scraping violates portal terms of service, real-time streaming ingestion is not possible at ₹0 without authorized broker or exchange market data feeds.

## 4. Part F: Prioritized 4-Tier Acquisition Roadmap

To ensure zero policy violations and maintain strict engineering discipline, future data acquisition (when authorized) must execute in four structured tiers:

### Tier 0: Policy Governance & Documentation Confirmation
- **Target:** Formal internal sign-off on personal academic research scope.
- **Action:**
  1. Document that all datasets stored locally are solely for private, non-commercial quantitative evaluation and algorithmic research.
  2. Implement repository-level `.gitignore` rules and storage encryption to guarantee zero accidental redistribution or public hosting of proprietary exchange files.

### Tier 1: Low-Risk, High-Value Open/Licensed Sources (Immediate Feasibility)
- **Target:** Domains C, I, L, M.
- **Action:**
  1. **Domain C & M (RBI DBIE):** Download official historical CSV exports for 91-Day T-Bills and Macro CPI/Repo rates directly from `data.rbi.org.in`.
  2. **Domain I (Point-in-Time Constituents):** Manually compile the historical NIFTY 50 reconstitution dates and symbol changes (2021–2024) from public NSE Indices press releases into `data/reference/nifty50_constituents_pit.csv`.
  3. **Domain L (Hugging Face 1m Options):** Acquire the `CC-BY-NC-4.0` Parquet shards from `thetrademarkk/india-index-options-1m` via Git LFS for local research evaluation.

### Tier 2: Semi-Annual & Periodic Manual Compilation (Controlled Ingestion)
- **Target:** Domains A, D, E, F, H, J, K.
- **Action:**
  1. **Domains A, D, F (Nifty Indices & VIX):** Execute manual batch queries on official web portals for 2021–2024 history, exporting standard CSV files to a staging directory.
  2. **Domains E, H, K (NSE Archives Bhavcopies):** Perform manual scheduled archive retrieval of historical EOD zip packages.
  3. **Ingestion Execution:** Run the offline validation engine specified in `P2.2.9_TECHNICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` to parse dual-schema files (Legacy vs UDiFF), harmonize lot sizes, verify price containment, compute internal breadth (Domain G), and generate provenance metadata.

### Excluded / Prohibited (Zero-Tolerance Boundaries)
- **Strictly Prohibited:**
  - Deployment of unthrottled, multi-threaded headless crawlers against `nseindia.com` or `niftyindices.com`.
  - Use of unofficial scraping wrappers (`yfinance`, reverse-engineered private mobile APIs).
  - Creation of paid vendor accounts, free-trial credential churning, or unauthorized broker API integration.
  - Commercial exploitation or public redistribution of acquired market datasets.
