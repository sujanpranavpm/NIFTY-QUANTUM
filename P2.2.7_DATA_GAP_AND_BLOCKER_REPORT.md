# P2.2.7 — Data Gap and Blocker Report

Date: 2026-11-07 · Milestone P2.2.7 (documentation-only). Inputs:
`P2.2.7_DATA_REQUIREMENTS_SPECIFICATION.md` (needs) and `P2.2.7_CURRENT_DATA_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md`
(measured coverage). This report says, for each research capability, **exactly which missing data
prevents it**, and separates an **ABSOLUTE BLOCKER** (the capability cannot be built honestly at all)
from a **QUALITY IMPROVEMENT** (the capability runs, but its realism/confidence is capped).

Constraint held throughout: **₹0, no accounts, no paid source, no downloads.** Where a field does not
exist in any permitted source, it is recorded as permanently unavailable — **not** reconstructed,
**not** guessed, **not** "implied".

## 1. Capability-by-capability blockers

### 1.1 Current options strategy research (the five Tier-1 long-only CE candidates)
- **Status: NOT blocked** on core data. Index daily/1m + dense option premium bars (1m/5m/15m) +
  effective-dated lot/tick/strike/expiry + charge schedule all exist for 2024+ and are present (unverified)
  for 2021-2023.
- **Raw files vs usable datasets:** 267 raw NIFTY option-expiry files were discovered and 801 derived candle
  files were created (267 × {1m, 5m, 15m}). However, exactly 265 expiry datasets and 795 candle files are
  usable: 2 expiry datasets (`2026-08-04` truncated tail stub and `2026-06-09` sparse sessions) are excluded by
  completeness and strict session gates, leaving 6 empty (0-row) candle files. Usable contract-day coverage is
  305,027 contract-days across 47,929 usable contracts (1,116 index-option overlap sessions).
- **Bounding limitation (QUALITY, not blocker):** the tradeable joint surface is short-DTE windows across the
  usable expiry set; each expiry is cached only for its final ~1-2 weeks. This caps **independent trade count /
  OOS density**, so the honest outcome of the P2.2.5/P2.2.6 screens was **"insufficient evidence — zero advance."**
  More market *history* (denser multi-week expiries), not more iterations, is the lever.
- **Pre-2024 sub-blocker (QUALITY):** 2021-2023 option cells are `UNKNOWN`/single-source (arbiter floor
  2024-01-02); they may be *simulated* but cannot be reconciled or published as verified.

### 1.2 Reliable options execution modelling
- **ABSOLUTE BLOCKER for spread/impact realism:** no historical **bid/ask (B10)**, no **spread (B11)**, no
  **order-book depth (B12)**, no **trade tape (B13)** exist at ₹0. Fills are bar-OHLC + assumed slippage bps;
  a crossed-spread or partial-fill simulation is **impossible**, not merely imperfect.
- **FIXABLE BLOCKER (behaviour, not data):** the exit-liquidity gate is `min_exit_volume=1.0` = **1
  share-equivalent**, so it rejects nothing even though volume is present (B2). This is a modelling defect
  to correct in a *later* authorised milestone; under the current "no strategy/execution modification" rule
  it is only **recorded** here.
- **FIXABLE BLOCKER (data present, unused):** expiry settles at the last premium close rather than the
  official `SttlmPric` (B6, available 2024+). Again recorded, not changed.

### 1.3 Strict financial validation
- **ABSOLUTE BLOCKER (fail-closed gate):** `readiness.py` `transaction_costs_verified` is open because the
  **2 slab-era `exchange_txn` rows pre-2024-10-01 are `estimated`** (H2). This is a *permanent unobservable*
  (the marginal slab a member pays is unknowable for a research book), **not a missing source** — so full-horizon
  strict validation can never close at ₹0. Trades dated **on/after 2024-10-01** are per-trade strict-verifiable
  with brokerage excluded.
- **Consequence:** results are correctly labelled **modelling-only / statutory-only**, never "net profit".
  This is a governance truth, not a gap to paper over.

### 1.4 Historical IV-aware strategy development
- **ABSOLUTE BLOCKER for observed-IV strategies:** no **observed contract IV (C2)** and no observed **Greeks (C3)**
  exist in the dataset. Missing observed historical contract IV prevents research that specifically requires actual
  historical IV observations. Contract selection today uses **only observable** spot/strike/expiry/premium/volume/OI
  (verified in `options/selection.py`: *"No Greeks, no IV, no future information"*).
- **Distinction from India VIX (C1):** missing India VIX prevents index-volatility-conditioned regime analysis,
  but **India VIX is NOT an input to Black-Scholes contract IV inversion**. An individual contract's IV is inverted
  from that contract's own premium, spot, strike, and time to expiry — index VIX and contract IV must not be conflated.
- **Model-derived IV is an estimate, not observed data:** contract IV could potentially be estimated by inverting
  an option pricing model (e.g. Black-Scholes) using observed premium (B1), spot (A1), strike/expiry (B4), a defensible
  **risk-free rate input (C4 — MISSING)**, and an index **dividend assumption (C5 — MISSING)**. Such IV and Greeks are
  **[DERIVED / ESTIMATED]** model outputs, not observed market data, must be recorded separately with model and input
  provenance (Rule H5), and are not equivalent to an observed volatility surface or suitable for every IV-aware strategy.
- **Candidate free official sources (for later discovery):** historical India VIX (C1) is published by NSE free of charge;
  risk-free benchmark yields (C4) are published by RBI/FBIL/CCIL; index dividend yield history (C5) is published by NSE Indices.
  These are identified for a later authorised discovery milestone (P2.2.8); no discovery or fetch is conducted here.

### 1.5 Realistic spread and liquidity modelling
- **ABSOLUTE BLOCKER** — same root as 1.2: B10–B13 unavailable at ₹0. Depth/bid-ask exist in **no permitted
  source** (P1 finding). No amount of in-repo derivation recovers a historical quote we never observed.

### 1.6 Market-regime analysis
- **PARTIAL — QUALITY BLOCKER:** a *price/vol* regime is available (`validation/regime.py`,
  `regime_ma_bars=200`, realised-vol from A4). But **vol-regime via VIX (C1)**, **breadth/sector regime (D6)**,
  and **participant/positioning regime (D1–D4)** are all MISSING, so regime conditioning is limited to
  index-price/vol features only. Not an absolute blocker for a price-regime study; a hard ceiling on a
  *volatility/positioning*-conditioned study.

### 1.7 Monte Carlo robustness testing
- **NOT blocked to *begin*,** because the primitives already operate on **backtest-generated simulated/derived** trade
  and daily return series (G1–G2) and i.i.d. bootstrap is implemented (`stats.py`).
- **Evidence classification:** market prices/quotes are [OBSERVED] inputs; historical backtest trade P&L (G1) and daily
  equity/portfolio returns (G2) are [SIMULATED] / [DERIVED] outputs from strategy rules, execution, and cost models.
  Actual live fills and realised account returns do not exist. The simulation engine resamples a finite set of
  backtest-generated outcomes, not raw observed market returns.
- **QUALITY BLOCKER:** statistical confidence is bounded by the **small independent sample of observed market days**
  (short-DTE option trades; 6-symbol equity smoke). **Block-bootstrap (G4) is not implemented** and consecutive-loss /
  drawdown-distribution breadth needs more OOS days. **Per this milestone MC is NOT to be implemented** — the
  requirement is the *data*, and the data gap is **history/sample size, not iteration count** (10,000 iterations do not
  create 10,000 independent historical observations).

### 1.8 Full-universe equity research (T10, `orders_17`)
- **ABSOLUTE BLOCKER for PIT-correct research:** point-in-time membership (F3) is **MISSING**
  (`index_membership_from/to` empty), delisted names (F6) and a dated corporate-action register (F4) are
  **MISSING**, so **survivorship bias is exposed, not corrected**. `orders_17` (requires PIT universe
  membership) **cannot run correctly**.
- **Fetch blocker (not a discovery blocker):** full NIFTY50 daily OHLCV (F1) is simply **not yet downloaded**
  (only ~6 symbols cached). This is the one gap closable at ₹0 by a future *authorised* yfinance fetch — but
  **no download is permitted in this milestone**, so it is recorded outstanding (T10).

## 3. What is essential vs merely nice-to-have

**Essential to *raise the confidence* of the current option study (highest ROI, all potentially ₹0):**
1. **Denser option history** — more multi-week life per expiry (reduce the short-DTE ceiling) and/or more
   expiries carried, subject to provider availability and the arbiter floor.
2. **Cost-gate resolution** — either an attested broker fee (moves to `all_in_verified`) or an accepted
   convention for the slab-era exchange rows; currently the only thing blocking *strict* validation.
3. **Fix the liquidity gate + wire `SttlmPric` settlement** (execution realism that needs **no new data** —
   volume and settlement price are already present). *Later authorised milestone; not touched here.*

**Essential to *unlock a new capability*:**
4. Point-in-time constituents + delisted + corporate actions (F3/F6/F4) → PIT-correct equity research, `orders_17`.
5. Full-universe daily OHLCV (F1) → breadth, sector, cross-sectional studies (₹0-fetchable, but not now).

**Nice-to-have (enrichment, largely NOT ₹0-obtainable):**
6. Historical bid/ask/depth (B10–B12) — realism ceiling, unavailable at ₹0.
7. Observed contract IV/Greeks (C2–C3) — not observed in the dataset; model derivation requires C4/C5 and yields estimated model outputs, not observed data. Observed India VIX (C1) is an index benchmark published by NSE free of charge, candidate for a future ₹0 fetch (P2.2.8) for regime conditioning.
8. Participant/FII/DII (D1–D4), macro (E1–E3) — supplementary context.

## 4. Honesty guardrails reaffirmed

- Absence of a field is reported as absence; **no missing data is invented**, and **no unavailable field is
  claimed reconstructable** to research grade (bid/ask, depth, observed IV, delisted history).
- **[DERIVED]** values (realised vol, a self-inverted IV/Greeks, a PCR from present-but-estimated OI) are
  permitted only when labelled derived with inputs recorded (H5), and never merged with **[OBSERVED]** series.
- Passing current quality checks ≠ having complete data. The gates confirm **internal consistency and
  fail-closed exclusion**; they do not certify market-wide completeness — which this audit shows is partial
  and pre-2024 unverifiable.
