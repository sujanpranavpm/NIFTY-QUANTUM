## 1. Owner's Clarified Access Policy & Core Reconciliation Purpose

Milestone `P2.2.9` previously evaluated candidate market-data sources under a generalized "no accounts of any kind" assumption. The repository owner has issued a formal policy clarification establishing non-negotiable access rules:

### 1.1 Non-Negotiable Access Rules
1. **COST (Absolute Zero-Cost):** The data and access route must be completely free at the point of access. Paid subscriptions, one-time dataset fees, paid APIs, commercial tiers, account deposits, or paid trials are strictly rejected.
2. **LOGIN (Existing Google Sign-In Allowed):** No new or separate provider-specific account, username/password, or developer registration may be required. An existing Google account sign-in (e.g., "Sign in with Google") is **acceptable**. Downloads hosted on Google Drive that require signing into an existing Google account are **acceptable**. No-login sources are also acceptable.
3. **GOOGLE-ONLY AUTHENTICATION:** If authentication is required, the sole acceptable route is an existing Google account sign-in. Do not create new Google accounts. Reject any source that additionally requires a separate provider account, broker/demat credentials, KYC, phone verification, or non-Google credentials.
4. **NO ACCOUNT-CYCLING / IDENTITY EVASION:** Do not create accounts, generate synthetic identities, bypass access controls, circumvent CAPTCHAs, or use borrowed/shared credentials.
5. **NO BROKER OR COMMERCIAL APIS:** Broker APIs and commercial vendor feeds remain excluded, even if they advertise limited free tiers or introductory trials.
6. **ACCESS ELIGIBILITY ≠ DATA-USE PERMISSION:** Passing the cost and login test makes a source **ACCESS-ELIGIBLE only**. Technical access does **not** grant legal permission to scrape, store, process, simulate with, or redistribute the data. Legal permissions must be evaluated independently with documentary evidence.

## 2. Two-Axis Classification Framework

To eliminate conflation between technical access feasibility and legal rights, all candidate sources are evaluated against two distinct, orthogonal axes:

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TWO-AXIS SOURCE EVALUATION TAXONOMY                                                                |
+--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| AXIS 1: ACCESS ELIGIBILITY                       | AXIS 2: DATA-USE PERMISSION                     |
+--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| PASS — NO LOGIN: Freely accessible w/o account.  | PERMITTED: Supported by explicit current terms. |
| PASS — GOOGLE LOGIN: Free via existing Google.   | CONDITIONAL: Requires consent, attribution, etc.|
| FAIL — PAYMENT: Any fee, subscription, or trial. | PROHIBITED: Explicit restriction applies.       |
| FAIL — NON-GOOGLE: Requires separate provider.   | UNCLEAR: Available terms do not settle rights.  |
| UNVERIFIED: Flow has not been confirmed.         | NOT APPLICABLE: Derived math (inherits inputs). |
+--------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
```

### Important Audit Principles:
- **No Conflation of Google Login with a License:** A file hosted on Google Drive or accessible via Google OAuth passes Axis 1, but does **not** inherit copyright permission from Google. The underlying content terms still apply.
- **Manual Download Does Not Imply Lawful Retention/Simulation:** Avoiding automated scraping via manual browser export does not automatically make local retention or quantitative backtesting lawful. If terms prohibit reproduction, storage in retrieval systems, or derived modeling, the permission status remains `CONDITIONAL` or `PROHIBITED`.
- **Derived Data Inherits Upstream Governance:** In-repo mathematical computations (BSM Greeks, market breadth) have zero external access gates, but strictly inherit the licensing and retention constraints of their upstream base inputs.

## 6. Treatment of Google Drive & Google OAuth Access

Under the owner's clarified rules:
1. **Google Drive Download Links:** If historical market data is hosted on Google Drive and requires the user to sign into their personal Google account, the source **`PASSES AXIS 1 (PASS — GOOGLE LOGIN)`**. It must not be rejected merely because Google authentication is requested.
2. **Authentication vs. Legal Title:** Signing into Google Drive or authenticating via Google OAuth establishes identity, but does **not** grant copyright or data usage rights. The actual data-use permission (Axis 2) is governed by the underlying licensor or curator. If a third party uploads copyrighted NSE data to Google Drive without authorization, downloading it remains legally precarious regardless of Google sign-in eligibility.
