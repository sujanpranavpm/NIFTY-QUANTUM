# P2.2.6 — Corrected Screening and Exit Evaluation

Date: 2026-11-06 · Baseline commit: `03a5d27` (HEAD unchanged) · Continues from the completed
**P2.2.5.1** screening-integrity audit · Budget: ₹0.

**Objective.** Replay the P2.2.5 screening under the **originally approved exit convention**
(TP **+15%** / SL **−8%** of entry premium; `orders_12` ratio-preserving **+16%/−8%**), document the
methodology deviation explicitly, and compare three exit series on **identical signals, contracts,
entry dates, sizing and costs** — changing only the exit rule. This is a *controlled research
replay*: no parameter was optimised or selected on results, no strategy or validation threshold
was touched, and the P2.2.5 artifacts are preserved verbatim.

> **Headline.** Under the approved band, **zero candidates advance — the classifications are
> unchanged** (4 × insufficient evidence, 1 × fails screening), but the deviation was **material**:
> the ±30% as-run band flattered the break-even gate for four of five candidates (`orders_12`
> +₹235 → −₹66; `orders_07` +₹161 → −₹79). The **signal-matched hold-to-expiry** comparison — the
> like-for-like benchmark the P2.2.5.1 audit called for — beats *every* TP/SL variant on *every*
> candidate (+13.7%…+25.9% vs −7.9%…+0.3% per unit premium). The exit overlay, not the signals, is
> where value is destroyed on this data.

## 10. Open decision referred back to the user

The replay deliberately does **not** re-ratify an exit convention. One explicit decision is needed:

1. **Adopt +15%/−8% (ratio-preserving) as the going-forward band** — the approved methodology, but
   on this data it is dominated by hold-to-expiry on every candidate; or
2. **Ratify ±30%/30% with a recorded approval** — retro-approving what P2.2.3 actually ran; or
3. **Change the exit *structure*** (e.g. hold-to-expiry as primary, wider asymmetric bands) —
   a methodology amendment, which the "never tuned to results" rule says should be argued on
   design grounds, not chosen from §3/§4 numbers.

Whichever is chosen should come with a correction of the P2.2.5 §1 "unchanged" label (an errata
note, not a rewrite of preserved results).
