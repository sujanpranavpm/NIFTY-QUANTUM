---

## 1. Governance, Standards & Research Rules

Every data source assessed in this inventory is evaluated under strict quantitative audit standards:
1. **Budget Constraint:** Strictly ₹0. Paid feeds, commercial subscriptions, trial accounts, and fee-bearing APIs are marked infeasible under project constraints.
2. **Authentication Constraint:** No accounts created, no registration forms submitted, no broker APIs utilized, and no personal credentials required.
3. **Provenance & Officialness:** First-party official publishers (NSE, NSE Indices, RBI, FBIL, CCIL) are prioritized. Third-party mirrors, scrapers, and open-source platforms are explicitly categorized as secondary leads, audited for terms, licensing, and reproducibility.
4. **Distinction of Rights:**
   - *Publicly viewable:* Data shown on a web page but not downloadable in bulk.
   - *Free to download:* Direct file export (CSV/ZIP) available without payment or login.
   - *Licensed for non-commercial research:* Explicit terms permitting academic, personal, or evaluation use.
   - *Openly redistributable:* Permissive open licenses (e.g. CC0, CC-BY, Public Domain).
5. **Format & Parser Continuity:** Structural format transitions (such as NSE's transition to the UDiFF ISO-20022 format on July 8, 2024) are documented with exact dates and field mappings.

### 2.1 NSE Historical India VIX Archive & API
- **Publisher / Operator:** National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE).
- **Direct URLs:**
  - Web UI: `https://www.nseindia.com/reports-indices-historical-vix`
  - Internal JSON API endpoint: `https://www.nseindia.com/api/historical/vixhistory?from={DD-MM-YYYY}&to={DD-MM-YYYY}`
  - Legacy historical reports archive: `https://www.nseindia.com/resources/historical-reports-capital-market-daily-monthly-archives`
- **Data Series / Report Name:** Historical Volatility Index — India VIX.
- **Granularity & Fields:** Daily.
  - Fields: `Date`, `Open`, `High`, `Low`, `Close`, `Previous Close`, `Change`, `% Change`.
- **Verified Coverage:**
  - Earliest Verified Date: **March 2, 2009** (historical inception of structured daily VIX dissemination).
  - Latest Verified Date: Current live trading date (continuous daily publication).
- **Update Frequency & Known Gaps:** Daily after session close (~16:00 IST). Zero known historical day gaps for regular trading sessions since inception.
- **Access Method & Cost:** Free, ₹0. Public web access via browser CSV download button.
  - Programmatic access requires standard browser headers (User-Agent) and session cookie initialization (warm-up GET request to `www.nseindia.com`) to pass CDN bot controls (Akamai). Date range per single API request is limited to 365 days, requiring annual chunking.
  - No login, no account creation, no API key required.
- **Licence & Terms:** Exchange public website terms. Intended for personal informational, research, and analytical use. Commercial redistribution prohibited without market data vendor agreement.
- **Research Utility:** Closes **Domain A (India VIX)** and P2.2.7 Requirement C1. Enables index volatility regime conditioning (e.g. VIX regime filters, vol-of-vol, sizing).
  - *Note:* India VIX is an index-level benchmark; it is **not** an input to individual option contract IV inversion.

  - Parsers must use `skiprows=1` to align column headers.
- **Licence & Terms:** Public clearing archive reports for regulatory transparency. Personal research use permitted.
- **Research Utility:** Closes **Domain E (Participant Positioning)** and P2.2.7 Requirements D1–D4. Allows net institutional positioning analysis (e.g., FII Long-Short Ratio in index futures, retail call writing vs institutional hedging).

### 2.3 NSE Daily F&O Bhavcopy — Post-July 2024 UDiFF Common Format
- **Publisher / Operator:** National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
- **Direct URL Pattern:**
  - `https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/fo/BhavCopy_NSE_FO_0_0_0_{YYYYMMDD}_F_0000.csv.zip`
- **Data Series / Report Name:** Daily F&O Common Bhavcopy (UDiFF Final).
- **Granularity & Fields:** Daily contract-level summary for all listed futures and options.
  - Headers (ISO-20022 compliant): `TradDt, BizDt, Sgmt, Src, FinInstrmTp, FinInstrmId, ISIN, TckrSymb, SctySrs, XpryDt, FininstrmActlXpryDt, StrkPric, OptnTp, FinInstrmNm, OpnPric, HghPric, LwPric, ClsPric, LastPric, PrvsClsgPric, UndrlygPric, SttlmPric, OpnIntrst, ChngInOpnIntrst, TtlTradgVol, TtlTrfVal, TtlNbOfTxsExctd, SsnId, NewBrdLotQty, Rmks, Rsvd1-4`.
- **Verified Coverage:**
  - Earliest Verified Date: **July 8, 2024** (the date UDiFF format officially replaced the legacy bhavcopy under NSE Circular No. 62424; files back-generated to 2024-01-01 exist on this endpoint).
  - Latest Verified Date: Current live trading date (318 files currently cached in repo under `data/raw/p1_nse/`).
- **Update Frequency:** Daily post-close (~18:00 IST).
- **Access Method & Cost:** Free, ₹0. Direct HTTP download via `nsearchives.nseindia.com`. Requires browser User-Agent header; supports HTTP `Range` probes.
- **Units & Conventions (authoritative):**
  - `TtlTradgVol` = contracts traded.
  - `OpnIntrst` = share-equivalents.
  - `NewBrdLotQty` = authoritative effective lot size.
  - `LastPric` = official last traded price (comparable to last minute bar close).
  - `SttlmPric` = derived exchange settlement price.
- **Licence & Terms:** Public exchange record; no copyright restrictions on internal research usage.
- **Research Utility:** Continues **Domain K (Daily F&O Bhavcopy)** for 2024+ onwards.

### 2.4 NSE Daily F&O Bhavcopy — Pre-July 2024 Legacy Historical Format
- **Publisher / Operator:** National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
- **Direct URL Pattern:**
  - `https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/historical/DERIVATIVES/{YYYY}/{MON}/fo{DD}{MON}{YYYY}bhav.csv.zip`
  - *(Example: `https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/historical/DERIVATIVES/2021/AUG/fo31AUG2021bhav.csv.zip`)*
- **Data Series / Report Name:** Legacy Derivatives Bhavcopy (`fo*bhav.csv.zip`).
- **Granularity & Fields:** Daily contract-level summary.
  - Legacy Headers: `INSTRUMENT, SYMBOL, EXPIRY_DT, STRIKE_PR, OPTION_TYP, OPEN, HIGH, LOW, CLOSE, SETTLE_PR, CONTRACTS, VAL_INLAKH, OPEN_INT, CHG_IN_OI, TIMESTAMP`.
- **Verified Coverage:**
  - Earliest Verified Date: **June 2000** (inception of index derivatives on NSE).
  - Latest Verified Date: **July 5, 2024** (final session before UDiFF format switchover on July 8, 2024).
- **Major Finding of P2.2.8:** In P1/P2, attempts to fetch pre-2024 bhavcopies using the UDiFF URL pattern returned 404, leading to the conclusion that pre-2024 was unavailable from NSE archives. This investigation confirms that **pre-2024 daily F&O bhavcopies exist and are publicly accessible on the legacy URL path** (`/content/historical/DERIVATIVES/`).
- **Access Method & Cost:** Free, ₹0. Direct HTTP GET with standard `User-Agent`. No login or session cookies required.
- **Schema Harmonization Required for Ingestion:**
  - `INSTRUMENT`: e.g. `OPTIDX` vs UDiFF `FinInstrmTp == 'IDO'`
  - `EXPIRY_DT`: `DD-Mon-YYYY` (e.g. `27-May-2021`) vs UDiFF `YYYY-MM-DD`
  - `STRIKE_PR`: float string vs UDiFF integer/float
  - `CONTRACTS`: traded contracts (= UDiFF `TtlTradgVol`)
  - `OPEN_INT`: contracts vs UDiFF share-equivalents (must be multiplied by lot size to reconcile with UDiFF `OpnIntrst`!)
- **Licence & Terms:** Public historical archive record.
- **Research Utility:** **High Impact.** Unlocks the ability to resolve the pre-2024 arbiter gap (2021-05 to 2023-12) in a future authorized ingestion milestone, eliminating the single-source `UNKNOWN` classification for 2021–2023 option days.

## 4. Primary Official Sources: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & FBIL

### 4.1 RBI Database on Indian Economy (DBIE) — Treasury Bill Auction Yields
- **Publisher / Operator:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Direct URLs:**
  - Portal: `https://data.rbi.org.in/`
  - Navigation: Financial Markets $\rightarrow$ Government Securities Market $\rightarrow$ Auctions of 91-Day, 182-Day, and 364-Day Government of India Treasury Bills.
- **Data Series / Report Name:** Treasury Bill Primary Auction Results.
- **Granularity & Fields:** Weekly auction results (held on Wednesdays).
  - Key Fields: `Auction Date`, `Notified Amount`, `Cut-off Price`, `Implicit Yield at Cut-off Price (Annualized %)`, `Weighted Average Yield (%)`.
- **Verified Coverage:**
  - Earliest Verified Date: Early 1990s (91-day T-bills), 1998 (364-day T-bills).
  - Latest Verified Date: Current auction week.
- **Access Method & Cost:** Free, ₹0. Downloadable directly in CSV/XLS format from the public portal without registration or API keys.
- **Tenor & Frequency Caveat:**
  - Auction frequency is **weekly**, not daily.
  - To use as a daily risk-free rate input in Black-Scholes option pricing, a standard quantitative convention is required: step-function (forward-filling the most recent Wednesday cut-off yield until the next auction) or linear interpolation between auctions.
- **Licence & Terms:** Official statistical publication of the central bank of India. Free for public research and economic analysis.
- **Research Utility:** Closes **Domain C (Risk-Free Rate Inputs)** and P2.2.7 Requirement C4. Supplies defensible sovereign short-term yields matching typical option expiries (91-day tenor).
